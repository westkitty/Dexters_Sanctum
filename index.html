A visitor’s guide

Drag the moon along its arc. Pick Stars and draw across the open sky. Touch the garden, lanterns, cottage or water to see what stirs. Drag elsewhere to look around. Click Dexter or press D for a closer look.

For keyboard play, choose a tool, then focus the scene. Arrow keys move the moon or star cursor. Enter plants a star, awakens the garden or touches the pool. Backspace removes the last star.

Postcard saves an image you can reopen. Choose Link to copy a living view or open a saved PNG. Dexter manages his own schedule.
